How could I forget to post about
one of the biggest events of my entire life?!&nbsp;
After living in the USA
for all of my &lt;mumblety-mumble&gt; years,
as of mid-July
I have MOVED TO PORTUGAL!!!1!!one!!

No, not bustling Lisbon,
nor the touristy Algarve,
nor the elite playground of Melides (as if I could afford it),
but the second largest city, Porto (aka Oporto).&nbsp;
Not the crowded hilly expensive historical district,
but more towards the center,
and the main Metro lines.&nbsp;
:-)&nbsp;
You can see the Casa da Música
and Rotunda da Boavista
from our apartment,
just a few blocks away,
and a tiny sliver of ocean on the horizon,
just a few miles away.

So, you may be wondering,
do I intend to
get involved in the local tech scene,
and/or keep speaking at conferences?&nbsp;
Yes and yes &mdash;
I'm retired,
so I don't have as much impetus to keep up on things
and will soon be even more behind the curve,
but there are some timeless ideas
(or old ones being rediscovered)
that I can talk about.&nbsp;
I am awaiting acceptance or rejection
from several conferences (as usual) already,
though the visa rules are playing merry hell with
my ability to commit to anything outside Portugal
between mid-October and maybe as far out as February.

And I intend to start brewing mead again,
and might find (or even start!) the local homebrewing scene.
